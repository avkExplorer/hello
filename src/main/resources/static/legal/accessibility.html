Accessibility Statement

Effective: 2026-11-25

We want hello to be usable by everyone. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 level AA.

What we do

  • Every release is scanned automatically with axe-core; serious and critical WCAG 2.1 A/AA problems stop the release.
  • Pages work with a keyboard and a screen reader, and respect your system colour and text-size settings.

Known limits

Automated scans find only some problems, and this statement is a draft. If something is hard for you to use, tell us.
